Welcome to Gemini Power Kit! This guide will help you set up and start using your new productivity toolkit.


## 1. Installation

If you haven't installed the extension yet, please follow our [Installation Guide](/guide/install/).

## 2. Open Gemini Power Kit Settings

<Steps>
1.  **Open Gemini**: Navigate to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com).
2.  **Find the entry**: At the bottom of Gemini's sidebar, select the **Gemini Power Kit** puzzle-piece icon beside Gemini's Settings icon. When the sidebar is collapsed, it appears immediately before **Settings & help**.
3.  **Open the panel**: Select the icon to open Gemini Power Kit settings. It opens to **Enhancements**, where you can turn optional features on or off.
</Steps>

### Choose and Enable Features

The settings panel groups **Quick Follow-up** and **Chain Prompt** under **Prompt**. Under **Tools**, use **Enhancements** to enable **Chat Outline**, **Bulk Delete**, and **Gem Avatar**, then open **Notifications**, **Shortcut**, or **Theme** for their dedicated controls.

## 3. Core Features at a Glance

### 🗂️ Chat Outline
Keep long chats clear with a clickable map of the current conversation.
- Jump directly to any prompt or response instead of scrolling through the whole chat.
- Pin the outline while you research or compare answers.

### ⚡ Quick Follow-up
Turn selected text from a Gemini response into your next prompt without copy-pasting.
- **Select text in a Gemini response** to open the Quick Follow-up capsule.
- Click **Ask Gemini** to attach the selected text as quote context above the input box, then type and send your follow-up manually.
- Click a saved **Custom Prompt** to replace **Selected Text** in the template and send it automatically.

### 🗑️ Bulk Delete
Clean up temporary Gemini chats in a deliberate batch while protecting the conversations you keep.
- Select 10, 20, 30, or 50 recent chats, then adjust individual selections if needed.
- Turn on **Exclude pinned** to leave pinned conversations out of a recent-batch selection.
- Review the selection and confirm before any chats are deleted.

### 🎨 Visual Themes and Chat Layout
Make Gemini easier and more comfortable to use.
- Choose color presets, use a custom wallpaper, and adjust the glass effect in **Theme**.
- Set chat and prompt widths in Default, %, or px; sync them, choose message alignment, or allow full-width messages with **Chat layout**.

### 🎭 Gem Avatars
Give each Gemini Gem a custom avatar, so it is easier to recognize in lists and chats.

### 🔔 Reply Notifications
Let Gemini work in the background and know when it is ready.
- Receive a desktop notification when a standard reply, image, video, or Deep Research report finishes.
- Open the originating Gemini chat from the notification and optionally use a built-in or custom sound.

### 🔗 Chain Prompt
Automate repetitive, multi-step tasks.
- Go to **Chain Prompt** in settings.
- Select a pre-defined chain or create your own sequence of prompts.
- Click **Run** and watch Gemini execute the steps one by one.

## 4. Pro Tips

- **Smart Tab Titles**: The extension automatically syncs your browser tab title with your chat name. No more guessing which tab is which!
- **Open Media in New Tab**: From the Library gallery, open an image or file in a new tab and keep your place in Gemini.
- **Privacy by Design**: Remember, everything runs locally. Your prompts and data are never sent to our servers.
