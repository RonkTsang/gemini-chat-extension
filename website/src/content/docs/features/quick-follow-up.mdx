**Quick Follow-up** lets you jot down a question beside the text, then keep reading. When you're ready, send your questions together with the original passages. You don't have to remember them all, find the text again, or explain which part you mean.

## 1. Select a Point and Click Ask Gemini

Open [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com) with Gemini Power Kit enabled. Select the passage you want to explore, then click **Ask Gemini** in the floating menu.

<img src={entryDemo.src} alt="Selecting text in a Gemini answer opens a floating menu with Ask Gemini and saved prompt icons" class="feature-image feature-image-narrow feature-image-full" />

*Selection entry — highlight the point you want to discuss, then choose **Ask Gemini**.*

## 2. Write Your Question Beside the Passage

A small comment field opens **beside the selected passage**, and a reference card appears above Gemini's main input. You can ask about that passage immediately, without scrolling down to write your question.

Type a question such as “Can you give a concrete example?”. Click the **checkmark** or press **Enter** to add it to the card without sending. Use **Shift+Enter** for a new line.

### Hold Command / Ctrl to Switch from Add to Send

The checkmark normally means **Add**. Hold **Command (⌘)** on macOS, or **Ctrl** on Windows/Linux, to change it to an upward **send arrow**. Release the key to return to the checkmark.

While holding that key, click the arrow, or press **⌘+Enter / Ctrl+Enter**, to add the current comment and send the complete draft. This sends **all** references, their comments, and the main request, including references added earlier.

| **Shift+Enter** | Start a new line |
| **⌘+Enter** / **Ctrl+Enter**, or hold the modifier and click the arrow | Add the comment and send the whole draft |

These shortcuts apply to a **new** comment. When reopening an existing comment, use **Save** to apply the change.

## 3. Collect a Separate Comment for Each Point

Keep reading, select another passage, and click **Ask Gemini** again. Write a question for that passage. Earlier cards, comments, and text in the main input stay in place, so you can collect your questions before sending. You can add up to **10 references**.

Comments and the overall request can be left empty. Card previews may be shortened to save space; the full selected text and comments are sent.

## 4. Review the References in Your Sent Message

After sending, the selected passages and their comments remain visible as cards in your message, alongside any overall request. You can look back at which question belonged to which passage while reading Gemini's answer.

Sent cards are read-only. The following editing controls apply to cards you are still preparing to send.

## Edit a Pending Comment from Its Card

1. In the main input, hover over a card with a comment and click its **edit icon**. You can also use the card's comment control.
2. Quick Follow-up returns to the source passage, highlights it, and opens the existing comment nearby.
3. Change the comment, then click **Save**. The matching card updates, while other cards and the overall request stay in place.

## Or Click the Numbered Marker Beside the Original Passage

Each pending reference also has a small numbered **marker** beside its source text. The number matches its card, so you can edit a question while reading the answer instead of finding the card first.

1. Find the marker beside the passage you want to discuss, such as **1**.
2. Hover over the marker to highlight the corresponding source passage.
3. Click the marker to reopen its comment beside the highlighted text.
4. Change your question and click **Save**. Check that the matching card shows the updated comment.

Choose **Cancel**, press **Esc**, or click outside to discard an unsaved edit. Editing a comment does not change the quoted passage. To remove a pending reference and its comment, use the card's **×** button; remaining cards are renumbered and the main request stays in place.

## Save Custom Prompts for Repeated Tasks

A **Custom Prompt** is a reusable instruction with a placeholder for the text you select. In settings, these prompts are managed as **skills**.

1. In Gemini's left navigation, open **Gemini Power Kit**.
2. Under **Prompt**, choose **Quick Follow-up**.
3. In **Skill Management**, click **Add Skill** (`+`).
4. Choose an icon, enter a name of up to 30 characters, and write your **Prompt Content**. Keep the **Selected Text** placeholder in the template; it is replaced with the passage you select.
5. Click **Save**. Back in the conversation, select text and choose the skill's icon.

For a translation skill, write “Translate [Selected Text] into Japanese and keep the original tone”, using the **Selected Text** placeholder in place of the brackets. For code review, try “Explain [Selected Text] step by step and identify edge cases”.

Use **Add Starter Templates** for **Tell me more**, **Search**, and **ELI5**, or reorder your skills to keep frequent actions first.

### What Happens When You Click a Skill?

| Current draft | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| No reference cards | The expanded prompt sends automatically. |
| Reference cards already added | Choose **Add to draft** or **Send separately**. |

**Add to draft** appends the expanded prompt to your main request and keeps your cards and comments. It does not send yet.

**Send separately** sends only that skill's expanded prompt, setting the reference draft aside and restoring it after Gemini clears the input. If the extension says the draft cannot be safely set aside, send or remove attachments and formatted content before trying again.

## Turn Quick Follow-up On or Off

Quick Follow-up is enabled by default. Open **Gemini Power Kit → Prompt → Quick Follow-up** and change **Enable Quick Follow-up**.

While enabled, it hides Gemini's built-in **Ask** button so you can use the extension's selection menu. Turning it off restores Gemini's button.

## Limits to Keep in Mind

- You can add up to **10 references** per draft. Remove one before adding another.
- Reference drafts belong to the current conversation. Switching conversations clears them; refreshing the page does not restore them.
- Returning to a source passage depends on that passage still being available in the current page. Do not rely on source markers surviving a reload.
- Review the references and comments before sending: they become part of the message Gemini receives.
