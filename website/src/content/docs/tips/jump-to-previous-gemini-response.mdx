When you’re halfway through a long Google Gemini answer and want to reread the reply before it, click **↑ Previous prompt** in Gemini Power Kit. It takes you to the question that started the previous turn. Gemini’s earlier answer is directly below it.

## How to go back one response in a long Gemini chat

1. [Install Gemini Power Kit](/guide/install/) and open a [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com) chat with several questions. Refresh the Gemini tab if it was open during installation.
2. While reading a reply, look in the upper-left corner of the conversation area for the **↑** and **↓** buttons beside the Chat Outline icon.
3. Click **↑** to jump to the previous question. Read the answer just below it. To go back another turn, click **↑** again.
4. Click **↓** to move forward one turn. You’ll land at the question that starts that turn.

## Example: Go back from a long follow-up to an earlier answer

Suppose you asked Gemini for a three-day itinerary, then asked for a hotel comparison, and finally requested a detailed budget. While reading the budget, click **↑** once to reach your hotel question and the comparison below it. Click **↑** again to reach the itinerary. From there, click **↓** twice to return to the budget question.

## Where are the arrows?

Open Gemini Power Kit from the puzzle-piece icon in Gemini’s sidebar and go to **Enhancements → Chat Outline**. Turn on **Chat Outline** and check **Show prompt navigation arrows** if that switch appears. The arrows appear in conversations that contain a prompt.

If you see **Scroll to top** when you hover over **↑**, you’re at the first loaded prompt. Click it to go to the top. Gemini may load more history as you scroll to the top of a long chat. If you want to pick a specific question several turns away, [open the Chat Outline list](/tips/find-earlier-gemini-prompts/) and select it directly.
