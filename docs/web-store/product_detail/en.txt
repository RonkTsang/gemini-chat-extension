Use Multi-Ask to collect questions about different passages and send them together. Customize your Gemini theme, use Chat Outline to jump to earlier prompts, and delete old chats in bulk.

THEMES, WALLPAPERS & LAYOUT
• Set your own wallpaper and matching colors by dropping an image onto the page outside the prompt box.
• Widen the chat to give code and tables more room. Adjust the prompt box width separately or match it to the chat.

MULTI-ASK — COLLECT QUESTIONS AS YOU READ
• Select passages as you read and write a separate question about each one.
• Send the questions together with their original passages in one message, without copying and assembling the context yourself.

CHAT OUTLINE
Find your way through long conversations. See an outline of your prompts, jump to an earlier turn, or keep the outline pinned while you work.

BULK DELETE CHATS
Select a batch of recent conversations, adjust the selection, and confirm deletion. Enable Exclude pinned to leave pinned chats out of the batch selection.

PROMPT CHAINS
Save repeatable multi-step tasks as reusable prompt chains. Use variables for different inputs, arrange steps in a drag-and-drop editor, and track progress as the chain runs.

REPLY NOTIFICATIONS
Enable desktop notifications to know when a reply, generated image or video, or Deep Research report is ready. Click a notification to return to its conversation.

MORE EVERYDAY TOOLS
• Give individual Gems custom avatars.
• Match browser tab titles to conversation titles.
• Open Library media in a new tab while keeping your place in the gallery.

PRIVACY
Gemini Power Kit processes its extension features in your browser and does not send your conversations to developer-controlled servers. Requests you submit to Gemini are handled by Google's services. Some preferences may sync through your browser account according to your browser settings.
