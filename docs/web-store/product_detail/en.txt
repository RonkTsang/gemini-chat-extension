Work faster, automate smarter, and reclaim your time.

Gemini Power Kit turns your AI chat into a high-performance workspace. 
Designed for those who demand more than just conversation, our toolkit eliminates repetitive manual work and provides the professional structure you need to stay focused.

KEY FEATURES
⚡ QUICK FOLLOW-UP — SELECT & ASK INSTANTLY
Eliminate the friction of copy-pasting. Interact with Gemini directly from any text you select.
  ● Instant Follow-up: Select any text and ask immediately.
  ● Custom Prompt Library: Build your own shortcuts like "Summarize" or "Explain" to run in one click.

🗂️ CHAT OUTLINE — KEEP LONG CHATS CLEAR
Stop scrolling endlessly through long threads.
  ● Instant Overview: See every prompt in your conversation at a glance.
  ● Jump and Pin: Go directly to any part of the chat, or keep the outline visible for research sessions.

🗑️ BULK DELETE — CLEAN UP GEMINI CHATS WITHOUT TOUCHING PINNED ONES
Clear temporary conversations in batches, while leaving the chats you pinned out of the cleanup.
  ● Exclude pinned chats in one action: Turn on Exclude pinned to skip pinned conversations when selecting a recent batch—no need to manually remove them from the selection.
  ● Delete the right-sized batch: Choose the 10, 20, 30, or 50 most recent chats, adjust individual selections if needed, then confirm and track deletion progress.

🎨 VISUAL THEMES — PERSONALIZE YOUR GEMINI
Transform your workspace and make Gemini truly yours.
  ● Stunning Color Presets: Choose from a variety of vibrant themes to create a more personal environment.
  ● Custom Wallpapers: Elevate your experience with personal backgrounds and elegant glass effects.
  ● Chat layout: Adjust Chat Width and Input Width in Default, % or px; sync them, choose message alignment, and allow full-width messages.

🔔 REPLY NOTIFICATIONS — KNOW WHEN GEMINI IS READY
Let Gemini work in the background without constantly checking the tab.
  ● Ready Alerts: Get a desktop notification when a standard reply, image, video, or Deep Research report is ready.
  ● One-Click Return and Sound: Open the originating Gemini chat from the notification, with an optional built-in or custom audio cue.

🔗 CHAIN PROMPT — AUTOMATE MULTI-STEP WORK
Turn multi-step tasks into one-click automations.
  ● Reusable Sequences: Execute prompt sequences with dynamic variables.
  ● Visual Control: Manage chains in a drag-and-drop editor and track progress step by step.

💎 SEAMLESS EXPERIENCE
● Smart Tab Titles: Automatically renames browser tabs to match your chat titles, making it effortless to switch between multiple Gemini sessions.
● Open Media in New Tab: From the Library gallery, open any item in a new tab and keep your place—review results without losing your scroll position.
