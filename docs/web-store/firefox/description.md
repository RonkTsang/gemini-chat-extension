Gemini Power Kit is a Firefox extension that helps you work with long Google Gemini chats. Use Multi-Ask to collect questions about different passages and send them together. Customize your Gemini theme, use Chat Outline to jump to earlier prompts, and delete old chats in bulk.

**KEY FEATURES**

**🎨 THEMES, BACKGROUNDS & LAYOUT**
Personalize Gemini and make room for your work.

- Set a custom background and matching colors by dropping an image onto the page outside the prompt box.
- Widen the chat for code and tables. Adjust the prompt box width separately or match it to the chat.

**💬 MULTI-ASK — COLLECT QUESTIONS AS YOU READ**
Keep reading without losing questions or context.

- Select passages as you read and write a separate question about each one.
- Send the questions together with their original passages in one message.

**🗂️ CHAT OUTLINE**
Find your way through long conversations.

- See an outline of your prompts and jump to an earlier turn.
- Keep the outline pinned while you work.

**🗑️ BULK DELETE CHATS**
Clear out old conversations in batches.

- Select a batch of recent conversations, adjust the selection, and confirm deletion.
- Exclude pinned chats from the batch selection.

**🔗 PROMPT CHAINS**
Save repeatable multi-step tasks as reusable prompt chains.

- Use variables for different inputs and arrange steps in a drag-and-drop editor.
- Track progress as the chain runs.

**🔔 REPLY NOTIFICATIONS**
Get a desktop notification when a reply, generated image or video, or Deep Research report is ready. Click it to return to the conversation.

**💎 MORE EVERYDAY TOOLS**

- Give individual Gems custom avatars and match browser tab titles to conversation titles.
- Open Library media in a new tab while keeping your place in the gallery.

**🔒 PRIVACY**
Gemini Power Kit processes its extension features in your browser and does not send your conversations to developer-controlled servers. Requests you submit to Gemini are handled by Google's services. Some preferences may sync through your browser account according to your browser settings.
